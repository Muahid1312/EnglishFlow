# LexiFlow original listening starters

These four original teaching scripts and synthetic recordings were created for
LexiFlow. LexiFlow is authorized to download, store privately, and play these
recordings for personal offline English study. They contain no third-party
provider recordings. This permission does not imply permission to download audio
from VOA, BBC, British Council, ELLLO, or other services.

Speech was synthesized with FFmpeg's Flite filter using the CMU SLT and RMS voice
models. Both are labeled as synthetic American English voices in the catalogue;
there is no claim of a human presenter or naturally recorded conversation.
`FLITE_COPYING.txt` records upstream software/voice notices, obtained from
https://github.com/festvox/flite/blob/master/COPYING. No Flite engine or voice
model binary is bundled in the Android application.

`lessons.json` records the actual audio duration, size, and SHA-256 digest.
The application catalogue pins published audio to an immutable Git commit.
B1/B2 are curriculum targets, not independently certified CEFR classifications.
